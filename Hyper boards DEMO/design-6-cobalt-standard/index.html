How it works

Build detail as confidence grows.

The process starts simply and becomes more detailed only when there is mutual reason to proceed.

Frequently asked

Useful answers before a first call.

Exploring a sale does not require a public process or a final decision.

01Do I need to be actively selling?

No. An owner can begin a conversation while still deciding whether, when, or how a transition should happen.

02What information is needed initially?

A broad description of the company, approximate financial profile, geography, and your objectives are enough for an initial fit discussion.

03Can I remain involved after a sale?

Possibly. The owner's role should reflect personal goals, transition needs, and what will serve the company.

04Are the financial ranges fixed?

No. They describe the current target profile, while any decision depends on the specific opportunity.

05Does Hyperboards represent sellers?

No. Hyperboards remains the buyer and does not act as a broker or M&A adviser marketing companies to others.
