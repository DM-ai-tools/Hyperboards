05 / Common questions

Clarity before
commitment.

Practical answers for owners who are considering what comes next.

Start a conversation ↗
Do I need to be actively selling my business?+

No. Many conversations begin while an owner is still considering timing and alternatives. You do not need a final decision to start a conversation.

Is the first conversation confidential?+

We intend to handle introductions discreetly. Highly sensitive documents should wait until appropriate protections and a secure process are in place.

What role can an owner have after a transaction?+

That depends on the owner’s goals and the company’s needs. A transition may involve an immediate handoff, a defined transition period, or a continuing role.

Are the published financial ranges absolute?+

No. They are current acquisition guidelines. Every company is evaluated on its specific facts; the ranges are not a valuation, financing commitment, or offer.

Is Hyperboards a broker or adviser?+

No. Hyperboards evaluates opportunities as the prospective buyer. We do not act as an investment bank or market businesses to third-party acquirers.
