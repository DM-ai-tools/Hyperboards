02 / The qualities that matterBeyond the headline numbers

Strong businesses
have more to them.

01

Repeat demand

Enduring customer needs, contracted relationships, subscriptions, maintenance, or dependable repeat purchasing.

02

Defensible earnings

Healthy economics, understandable adjustments, sensible capital needs, and a track record that can be responsibly underwritten.

03

Transferable capability

A capable team, documented routines, and durable supplier and customer relationships that can survive a transition.

04

Market relevance

A useful reputation, niche expertise, attractive local density, or another practical reason customers choose the business.

05

Responsible transition

An owner willing to share context and define a handover that considers the people and relationships behind the company.

06

Considered potential

Room to improve systems, expand responsibly, or strengthen the business while understanding what already works.
