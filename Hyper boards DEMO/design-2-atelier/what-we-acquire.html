What tends to fit

Substance beyond the headline numbers.

Strong fit usually comes from a combination of repeat demand, capable operations, and a company that can transfer successfully beyond its current owner.

01

Recurring or repeat customers

Revenue supported by enduring customer needs, contracted relationships, subscriptions, maintenance, or dependable repeat purchasing.

02

Defensible earnings

Healthy economics with understandable adjustments, sensible capital needs, and a track record that can be responsibly underwritten.

03

Transferable capability

A capable team, documented routines, durable supplier or customer relationships, and knowledge that can survive a transition.

04

Clear market relevance

A useful reputation, niche expertise, attractive local density, or another practical reason customers continue to choose the business.

05

A responsible transition

An owner willing to share context and help define a handover that protects the people and relationships behind the company.

06

Room for considered growth

Opportunities to improve systems, expand responsibly, or strengthen the business without discarding what already works.

Current industry focus

Nine sectors selected for repeat demand.

Industry is a starting point. We look for attractive economics, transferable operations, and a strong underlying business in every category.

Building & Construction

Project backlogs, maintenance needs, and repeat commercial relationships can support durable demand.

Communication & Media

Contracted engagements and recurring client relationships can create visible, repeatable economics.

Entertainment & Recreation

Membership, participation, and recurring-use models may produce dependable customer behavior.

Financial Services

Recurring revenue and transferable books of business can support continuity beyond the owner.

Health Care & Fitness

Defensive demand and recurring service relationships can remain durable through operating cycles.

Manufacturing

Established customer relationships, operating know-how, and tangible assets can support transferability.

Online & Technology

Scalable operations and repeat usage can create attractive economics when customer demand is durable.

Service Businesses

Fragmented service categories may offer resilient local demand and thoughtful consolidation potential.

Wholesale & Distributors

Recurring B2B needs and long-standing supplier relationships can underpin stable operating performance.

Sector inclusion does not imply fit. Every opportunity is assessed against the full acquisition and underwriting criteria.

Outside our current mandate

Agriculture; automotive and boats; beauty and personal care; education and children; pet services; restaurants and food; retail; transportation and storage; and travel.
