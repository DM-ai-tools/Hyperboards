Role declaration / 02

Our role

We are the buyer—
not the adviser finding one.

We evaluate businesses as a potential acquirer. You speak with the people considering the purchase, not an intermediary assembling a buyer list.

That makes the relationship simpler: owner to buyer, question to answer, conviction to decision.

Working principles / 04

A transaction can be exacting without becoming impersonal.

Ⅰ

Direct engagement

Speak with the prospective buyer from the beginning, with fewer layers between context and decision.

Ⅱ

Discretion first

Early conversations stay narrow, practical, and sensitive to employees, customers, and counterparties.

Ⅲ

Flexible transitions

Owner involvement after closing is discussed around what the business and the owner actually need.

Ⅳ

Respect for the work

We begin by understanding what made the company durable before imagining what should change.

A considered process / 05

Clarity at every signature.

A business should be understood before terms are finalized. Our process builds detail as mutual interest grows.

  1. 01

    Confidential introduction

    Share only the context needed to determine mutual fit.

  2. 02

    Initial fit

    Review the operating profile, owner priorities, and broad economics.

  3. 03

    Business review

    Develop a shared understanding of customers, operations, and risk.

  4. 04

    Terms and diligence

    Align on structure, then verify the information supporting it.

  5. 05

    Closing and transition

    Document the agreement and prepare a deliberate handover.
