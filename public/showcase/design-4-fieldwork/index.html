Ask us directly ↗
Do I need to be actively selling my business?+

No. Many conversations begin while an owner is still considering timing and alternatives. A first discussion can help you explore whether there is a fit, without an obligation to proceed.

Is the first conversation confidential?+

We intend to handle introductions discreetly. Highly sensitive documents should wait until appropriate protections and a secure process are in place.

What role can I have after a transaction?+

That depends on your goals and the company’s needs. A transition may involve an immediate handoff, a defined period, or a continuing role. We consider this together in the context of the business.

Are the published financial ranges absolute?+

No. They are current acquisition guidelines, and every company is evaluated on its specific facts. They are not a valuation, financing commitment, or offer.

Is Hyperboards a broker or adviser?+

No. Hyperboards evaluates opportunities as the prospective buyer. We do not act as an investment bank or market businesses to third-party acquirers.
