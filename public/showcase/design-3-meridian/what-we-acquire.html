What tends to fit

The strength
beneath
the numbers.

We look at the business as a whole: how it earns, why customers return, and what enables it to continue.

01

Recurring or repeat customers

Revenue supported by enduring needs, contracted relationships, subscriptions, maintenance, or dependable repeat purchasing.

02

Defensible earnings

Healthy economics, understandable adjustments, sensible capital needs, and a track record that can be responsibly underwritten.

03

Transferable capability

A capable team, documented routines, and durable relationships, with knowledge that can survive a transition.

04

Clear market relevance

Niche expertise, a useful reputation, attractive local density, or another practical reason customers continue to choose the business.

05

A responsible transition

An owner willing to share context and help define a handover that respects the people and relationships behind the company.

06

Room for considered growth

Potential to improve systems, expand responsibly, or strengthen the business while preserving what already works.
