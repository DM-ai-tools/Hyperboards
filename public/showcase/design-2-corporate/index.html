Common questions

A clearer
starting point.

A first conversation can answer practical questions without creating pressure to proceed.

Do I need to be actively selling my business?

No. Many conversations begin while an owner is still considering timing and alternatives. You do not need a final decision to introduce yourself.

Is the first conversation confidential?

We intend to handle introductions discreetly. Highly sensitive documents should wait until appropriate protections and a secure process are in place. An introduction alone does not create an NDA.

What role can I have after a transaction?

That depends on your goals and the company’s needs. A transition may involve an immediate handoff, a defined transition period, or a continuing role.

Are the financial ranges absolute?

No. The $750K–$2M target EBITDA and $2M–$6M typical deal value are current acquisition guidelines. Every company is evaluated on its specific facts, and these ranges are not a valuation or offer.

Is Hyperboards a broker or adviser?

No. Hyperboards evaluates opportunities as the prospective buyer. We do not market your business to third-party acquirers or run a sale process on your behalf.
