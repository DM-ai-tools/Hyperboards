Before we begin

A little
more clarity.

A first conversation can answer practical questions without creating pressure to proceed.

Do I need to be actively selling my business?

No. Many conversations begin while an owner is still considering timing and alternatives. An introduction does not create an obligation to proceed.

Is the first conversation confidential?

We intend to handle introductions discreetly. Highly sensitive documents should wait until appropriate protections and a secure process are in place.

What role can I have after a transaction?

That depends on your goals and the company’s needs. A transition may involve an immediate handoff, a defined transition period, or a continuing role.

Are your financial ranges absolute?

No. They are current acquisition guidelines, and every company is evaluated on its specific facts. Actual value depends on earnings quality, transaction structure, and diligence.

Is Hyperboards a broker or adviser?

Hyperboards evaluates each opportunity as the direct prospective buyer. We do not market businesses to third-party acquirers or run an auction on your behalf.
