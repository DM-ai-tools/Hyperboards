The acquisition mandate

Good businesses.
Enduring qualities.

We look for established, profitable companies with the people, customer relationships, and operating strengths to support a thoughtful transition.

The buyer’s perspectiveWe evaluate each opportunity for ourselves.

Hyperboards is the direct prospective buyer. We do not act as a broker, investment bank, or adviser representing another party.

The qualities behind the numbers

Substance first.
Always.

Sector and size begin the conversation. The quality of the underlying business determines whether there is a fit.

01

Recurring or repeat customers

Enduring customer needs, contracted relationships, subscriptions, maintenance, or dependable repeat purchasing.

02

Defensible earnings

Healthy economics with understandable adjustments, sensible capital needs, and a track record that can be responsibly underwritten.

03

Transferable capability

A capable team, documented routines, and durable relationships that can survive an ownership transition.

04

Clear market relevance

Niche expertise, a useful reputation, attractive local density, or another practical reason customers continue to choose the business.

05

A responsible transition

An owner willing to share context and help define a handover that protects the people and relationships behind the company.

06

Room for considered growth

Opportunities to improve systems, expand responsibly, or strengthen the business while preserving what already works.
