Start with the broad outline.

A brief email is enough to begin. Tell us about your business and what you are considering, in your own words.

  1. Your business

    Its name, location, industry, and what it does.

  2. Your perspective

    Your role, your priorities, and the timing you have in mind.

  3. The broad picture

    Share approximate financial context if you are comfortable, or leave it for a conversation.
