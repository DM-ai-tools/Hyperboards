What tends to fit

Substance beyond the headline numbers.

Strong fit usually comes from a combination of repeat demand, capable operations, and a company that can transfer successfully beyond its current owner.

01 / Demand

Recurring or repeat customers

Revenue supported by enduring customer needs, contracted relationships, subscriptions, maintenance, or dependable repeat purchasing.

02 / Quality

Defensible earnings

Healthy economics with understandable adjustments, sensible capital needs, and a track record that can be responsibly underwritten.

03 / Operations

Transferable capability

A capable team, documented routines, durable supplier or customer relationships, and knowledge that can survive a transition.

04 / Position

Clear market relevance

A useful reputation, niche expertise, attractive local density, or another practical reason customers continue to choose the business.

05 / Stewardship

A responsible transition

An owner willing to share context and help define a handover that protects the people and relationships behind the company.

06 / Potential

Room for considered growth

Opportunities to improve systems, expand responsibly, or strengthen the business without discarding what already works.
