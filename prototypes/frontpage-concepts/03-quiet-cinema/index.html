Stewardship / three scenes

After the signature,
the real story continues.

A company is held together by more than reported earnings. We look for the knowledge, relationships, and habits that make the numbers possible.

01 / PEOPLE

Listen before directing.

The people closest to the work often hold the context a spreadsheet cannot show. Understanding begins there.

02 / CUSTOMERS

Protect earned trust.

Customer relationships compound through reliability. A transition should respect the expectations the business already fulfills.

03 / CONTINUITY

Change with a reason.

We do not assume every new owner idea is an improvement. The operating foundation deserves to be understood first.

The transaction / in sequence

Clear steps.
No unnecessary theatre.

  1. 01

    Confidential introduction

    A narrow first exchange to establish fit, owner priorities, and timing.

  2. 02

    Initial fit

    A review of operations and broad financial characteristics.

  3. 03

    Business review

    A closer understanding of people, customers, systems, and dependencies.

  4. 04

    Terms & diligence

    Agreement on a practical structure, followed by verification.

  5. 05

    Closing & transition

    Final documentation and a handover shaped around the business.
