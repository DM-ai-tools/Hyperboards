05 / Before we beginClarity first

A few practical questions.

A first conversation can create clarity without creating pressure to proceed.

01Do I need to be actively selling my business?+

No. Many conversations begin while an owner is considering timing and alternatives.

02Is the first conversation confidential?+

We intend to handle introductions discreetly. Sensitive documents should follow appropriate protections and a secure process.

03What role can an owner have after a transaction?+

That depends on the owner's goals and the needs of the company. Different transition paths may be considered.

04Are the published ranges absolute?+

No. They are current acquisition guidelines, and every company is evaluated on its specific facts.

05Is Hyperboards a broker or adviser?+

No. Hyperboards evaluates opportunities as the prospective buyer and does not market businesses to third-party acquirers.
